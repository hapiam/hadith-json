rule picked a winner. Left in the data as-is (both rows kept) pending manual

They found Amr bin Al-Aas, and said to him, "O son of Al-Aas, seek permission for us to see the Commander of the Faithful." He replied, "By Allah, you have both hit the mark, for his name is the Commander, and we are the believers."
