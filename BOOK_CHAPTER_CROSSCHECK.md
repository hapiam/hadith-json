## Bukhari / Nasa'i / Abu Dawud: real, narrow disagreements at book boundaries

Every mismatch clusters in a short citation range, always off by exactly
one adjacent book number (e.g. Bukhari citations 4474–4485: amrayn says
book 64 "Military Expeditions," canonical says book 65 "Prophetic
Commentary on the Qur'an"). This is not a bug in either source — it's a
genuine editorial disagreement between amrayn/sunnah.com's digitization
and fawaz's own digitization about precisely which hadith is the first of
the next book. Both sides agree on content and order; they disagree by a
handful of citations on exactly where the boundary line falls. Small and
localized (0.2–7.3% of each book), not evidence either source is wrong at
scale.

## Muslim: this comparison method doesn't work for this book

67% of Muslim's citations (5,969/8,898) are lettered (`8a`/`8b`/`8d`...),
which this comparison skips entirely — amrayn (Type 2, grouped-lettered)
and fawaz/canonical (Type 1, flat sequential) count Muslim differently at
a structural level (documented in `SOURCE_REGISTRY.md`), so a citation's
leading integer doesn't reliably line up with canonical `idInBook` the
way it does for the other 3 books. Most of the "mismatches" reported for
Muslim are citations 8–83 landing in canonical's `chapterId: 0`
("Introduction," 83 entries appended outside fawaz's numbered scheme —
see main `README.md`'s "Known limitations") vs. amrayn's book 1. This
number is not a reliable finding as-is — a real Muslim comparison would
need the same content-based matching `tool/arabic_match.dart` already
uses elsewhere in this repo, not citation-number matching. Not attempted
here.
